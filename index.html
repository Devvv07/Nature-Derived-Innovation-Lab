The Biomimicry Process

Observe natural systems, formalize performance models, prototype with engineering tools, and validate with data.

  1. Discover — Field observation anchored in engineering criteria.
  2. Abstract — Formal mathematical models and systems maps.
  3. Emulate — CAD, robotics and materials prototypes.
  4. Evaluate — Quantitative validation with analysis and simulation.

The Labs

Dual deployment for advanced research and rural outreach

NDIL combines a state-of-the-art urban hub with a solar-powered mobile lab to deliver applied biomimicry across industry, academics and underserved communities.

Vadodara Innovation Hub

A controlled facility designed to convert natural design into industrial systems through observation, analysis, prototyping and demonstration.

  • Observation Atrium — Comparative study of plant hydraulics, thermal regulation and form-function systems.
  • Analysis Bay — MATLAB/Python workstations, FEA and performance analytics.
  • Prototyping Workshop — CNC, additive manufacturing, sensors and modular robotics.
  • Pitch Arena — Industry-grade presentation space for investor and recruiter evaluations.

Mini-factory Simulation

Simulation spaces mirror robotics workflows, CAD-driven assembly and industrial packaging to teach production engineering through nature-based examples.

BioLab-on-Wheels

A 100% solar-powered mobile lab that delivers rigorous STEM training to tribal and agrarian regions using local ecological systems as the teaching platform.

  • 100% off-grid and self-sufficient
  • Solar-powered classroom and lab systems
  • Hyper-local curriculum built from regional ecosystems
  • Designed for rural schools, tribal belts and MSME clusters
